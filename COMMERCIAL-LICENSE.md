# Commercial license

SuperGhidra is free software under the [GNU AGPL-3.0-or-later](LICENSE). That license is fine for most uses, including internal use, research and open-source projects that are themselves AGPL-compatible.

You may want a commercial license if you:

- embed SuperGhidra in a product you distribute and can't release that product's source under the AGPL
- run a modified SuperGhidra as a hosted service and can't offer users the modified source
- work under a company policy that doesn't allow AGPL dependencies

A commercial license replaces the AGPL terms for the code you license. It is a written agreement, so terms such as scope, support and price are settled case by case. There is no standard price list yet.

## How to ask

Open an issue titled "Commercial license" at https://github.com/yuatemycat/SuperGhidra/issues. Say what you want to build and roughly how it would be distributed. Don't put anything confidential in a public issue; I'll move the details to a private channel.

## What this does not cover

- Ghidra, Z3, WGPU and the other dependencies keep their own licenses. A SuperGhidra commercial license doesn't change them.
- Releases up to 0.2.0 were published under Apache-2.0. You can keep using those under Apache-2.0 without any agreement with me.
